2. Multithreading — Four Lines

Every case runs four parallel stakeholder lines. You orchestrate; specialists execute.

Economic Buyer

CIO / CFO — cares about cost, risk, business outcome
Pitch: consolidation, TCO, time-to-value, regulatory exposure

Technical Evaluator

Head of IT Ops / Infrastructure — cares about feasibility, migration risk
Pitch: no downtime, hybrid visibility, mainframe coverage

Champion

Servicedesk lead / power user — feels the daily pain
Pitch: UX, fewer tools, faster workflows — they sell internally for you

Compliance / Risk

Audit, DSGVO, DORA contacts
Pitch: audit trails, data residency, evidence — engaged early so it never becomes a blocker

Partner layer (Atlassian-specific)

Atlassian sells 100% through partners. Partners run implementation and on-site workshops — something an Atlassian AE cannot do directly. At Eventim and the media conglomerate, the partner co-led the PoC. Frame it as: "I own the commercial motion and stakeholder map; the partner brings implementation depth I cannot replicate solo."

4. Lost Deal — The Honest Gap

JSM loses in regulated environments where Atlassian's cloud-first strategy doesn't fit. This is not a failure story — it's the bridge to BMC.

What happened

  • Large regulated customer (bank / insurer) needed on-prem or hybrid — could not move 100% to cloud
  • Atlassian Data Center is EOL 2029; cloud-first is non-negotiable for us
  • JSM could not meet the data-residency / control requirement
  • Deal lost to a vendor with native hybrid + on-prem depth

Why this matters

Many large European banks and insurers still require hybrid. DORA demands full visibility of dependencies — you can't assess risk you can't see. If the platform can't run where the data lives, the conversation ends.

Why this is my reason to move

I have seen this gap repeatedly from the Atlassian side. BMC Helix offers cloud, on-prem and hybrid natively — exactly the deployment flexibility these accounts need. I want to sell into that space, not lose deals to it.

"Atlassian is strong on DevOps and land-and-expand. But in regulated financial services, hybrid visibility and operational resilience go further than JSM can reach today. That's the gap I want to close — at BMC."
